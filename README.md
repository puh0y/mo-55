\# MO-55



A moss-covered robot with glowing LED-ring eyes that reacts to sound, light, and movement.



\## Status

Parts are gathered and sorted. Arduino IDE is set up and my first upload works. Next up is wiring the first sensors, and I'm waiting on the LED rings and a base box.



\## Parts

\- Arduino Uno R3

\- LED rings (eyes)

\- 2 sound sensors

\- HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor

\- PIR motion sensor

\- Photoresistor

\- MPU-6050 accelerometer

\- SG90 servo

\- Passive buzzer

\- LCD1602 screen

\- Buttons, IR receiver and remote



\## How it works

MO-55 listens, watches, and feels movement through its sensors, then reacts with moods shown in his eyes and heard in his sounds.
